And so the cycle begins again.

Long has it been since humans have been down this path. The worlds themselves have changed. Some are gone. Some have been born anew.  Even the Earth, ever present, unrecognizable to those that saw it in its younger days.

So long has it been that the humans have forgotten how this calamity came to be, time and time again. Their own history has lost the story of why it happened. Of how it happened. Of how they brought it upon themselves. Only time will tell if they learn this time, for all time.

But time, to us, is meaningless.

We remember.
